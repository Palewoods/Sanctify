5	Bethel,—Galgal,—Bersabee.... The places where they worshipped their idols.
8	Arcturus and Orion.... Arcturus is a bright star in the north: Orion a beautiful constellation in the south.
9	With a smile.... That is, with all ease, and without making any effort.
25	Did you offer, etc.... Except the sacrifices that were offered at the first, in the dedication of the tabernacle, the Israelites offered no sacrifices in the desert.
26	A tabernacle, etc.... All this alludes to the idolatry which they committed, when they were drawn away by the daughters of Moab to the worship of their gods. Num. 25.
