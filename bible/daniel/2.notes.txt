1	The second year.... Viz., from the death of his father Nabopolassar; for he had reigned before as partner with his father in the empire.
2	The Chaldeeans.... That is, the astrologers, that pretended to divine by stars.
39	Another kingdom.... Viz., that of the Medes and Persians. Ibid. Third kingdom.... Viz., that of Alexander the Great.
40	The fourth kingdom, etc.... Some understand this of the successors of Alexander, the kings of Syria and Egypt, others of the Roman empire, and its civil wars.
44	A kingdom.... Viz., the kingdom of Christ in the Catholic Church which cannot be destroyed.
