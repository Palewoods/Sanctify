3	A large eagle.... Nabuchodonosor, king of Babylon.—Ibid. Came to Libanus.... That is, to Jerusalem.—Ibid. Took away the marrow of the cedar.... King Jechonias.
4	Chanaan.... This name, which signifies traffic, is not taken here for Palestine, but for Chaldea: and the city of merchants here mentioned is Babylon.
5	Of the seed of the land, etc.... Viz., Sedecias, whom he made king.
6	Towards him.... Nabuchodonosor, to whom Sedecias swore allegiance.
7	Another large eagle.... Viz., the king of Egypt.
12	Shall take away.... Or, hath taken away, etc., for all this was now done.
22	Of the marrow of the high cedar, etc.... Of the royal stock of David.—Ibid. A tender twig.... Viz., Jesus Christ, whom God hath planted in mount Sion, that is, the high mountain of his church, to which all nations flow.
