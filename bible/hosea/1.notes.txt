2	A wife of fornications.... That is, a wife that has been given to fornication. This was to represent the Lord’s proceedings with his people Israel, who, by spiritual fornication, were continually offending him.—Ibid. Children of fornications.... So called from the character of their mother, if not also from their own wicked dispositions.

9	Not my people.... Lo-ammi.
10	The number, etc.... Viz., of the true Israelites, the children of the church of Christ.
