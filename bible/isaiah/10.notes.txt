22	A remnant of them shall be converted.... This was partly verified in the children of Israel who remained after the devastations of the Assyrians, in the time of king Ezechias: and partly in the conversion of a remnant of the Jews to the faithful of Christ.—Ibid. The consumption abridged, etc.... That is, the number of them cut short, and reduced to few, shall flourish in abundance of justice.
27	At the presence of the oil.... That is, by the sweet unction of divine mercy.
28	Into Aiath, etc.... Here the prophet describes the march of the Assyrians under Sennacherib; and the terror they should carry with them; and how they should suddenly be destroyed.
