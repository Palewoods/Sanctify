1	Edom.... Edom and Bosra (a strong city of Edom) are here taken in a mystical sense for the enemies of Christ and his church.
15	They have held back, etc.... This is spoken by the prophet in the person of the Jews at the time when, for their sins, they were given up to their enemies.
16	Abraham hath not know us, etc.... That is, Abraham will not now acknowledge us for his children, by reason of our degeneracy; but thou, O Lord, art our true father and our redeemer, and no other can be called our parent in comparison with thee.
17	Made us to err, etc. Hardened our heart, etc.... The meaning is, that God in punishment of their great and manifold crimes, and their long abuse of his mercy and grace, had withdrawn his graces from them, and so given them up to error and hardness of heart.
