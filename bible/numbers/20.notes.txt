11	The rock.... This rock was a figure of Christ, and the water that issued out from the rock, of his precious blood, the source of all our good.
12	You have not believed, etc.... The fault of Moses and Aaron, on this occasion, was a certain diffidence and weakness of faith; not doubting of God’s power or veracity; but apprehending the unworthiness of that rebellious and incredulous people, and therefore speaking with some ambiguity.
13	The Water of contradiction.... Or strife. Hebrew, Meribah.
