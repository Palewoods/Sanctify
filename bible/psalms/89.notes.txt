3	Turn not man away, etc.... Suffer him not quite to perish from thee, since thou art pleased to call upon him to be converted to thee.
9	As a spider.... As frail and weak as a spider’s web; and miserable withal, whilst like a spider we spend our bowels in weaving webs to catch flies.
10	Mildness is come upon us, etc.... God’s mildness corrects us; inasmuch as he deals kindly with us, in shortening the days of this miserable life; and so weaning our affections from all its transitory enjoyments, and teaching us true wisdom.
