Lesson 17: On Indulgences
? 231. What is an Indulgence?
An Indulgence is the remission in whole or in part of the temporal punishment due to sin.
? 233. How many kinds of Indulgences are there?
There are two kinds of Indulgences—Plenary and Partial.
? 234. What is a Plenary Indulgence?
A Plenary Indulgence is the full remission of the temporal punishment due to sin.
? 235. What is a Partial Indulgence?
A Partial Indulgence is the remission of a part of the temporal punishment due to sin.
? 237. What must we do to gain an Indulgence?
To gain an Indulgence we must be in the state of grace and perform the works enjoined.
