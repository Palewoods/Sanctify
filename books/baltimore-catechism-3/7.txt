An Act of Hope
? 97. Say the Act of Hope.
O my God! relying on Thy infinite goodness and promises, I hope to obtain pardon of my sins, the help of Thy grace, and life ever-lasting, through the merits of Jesus Christ, my Lord and Redeemer.
? 98. Give the substance of an Act of Hope.
The substance of an Act of Hope is: I hope for heaven and the means to obtain it.
