The Salve Regina
Hail to the Queen who reigns above,	Mother of clemency and love;	Hail thou, our hope, life, sweetness; we,	Eve's banish'd children, cry to thee.	We, from this wretched vale of tears,	Send sighs and groans unto thy ears;	O then, sweet Advocate! bestow	A pitying look on us below.	After this exile let us see	Our blessed Jesus, born of thee.	O merciful, O pious Maid,	O gracious Mary, lend thy aid.	V. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God;	R. That we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ.
