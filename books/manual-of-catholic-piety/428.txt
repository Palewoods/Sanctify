Hymn at the Elevation
O salutaris hostia, O saving host, that heaven's gate, Quæ cœli pandis ostium; Laidst open at so dear a rate; Bella premunt hostilia, Intestine wars invade our breast; Da robur, fer auxilium. Be thou our strength, support, and rest. Uni trinoque Domino, To God the Father, and the Son, Sit sempiterna gloria: And Holy Spirit, three in one, Qui vitam sine termino, Be endless praise: may He above. Nobis donet in patria. With life eternal crown our love.
